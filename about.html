Our Story

A Business Graduate turned Global Sourcing Expert

Meet Asad Sheikh, the founder of Merx Trading. A BBA student who started his journey in China sourcing and trading in 2018, Asad built his experience from the ground up by working closely with Chinese suppliers, factories, and the trading ecosystem. What started as an interest in international trade gradually became a deep understanding of how business is done on the ground in China.

Over the years, he developed a strong understanding of the Chinese market, its suppliers, manufacturing processes, and business culture. His experience allows him to connect global buyers with reliable Chinese factories while navigating the complexities of sourcing with confidence, transparency, and precision.

We are not a faceless sourcing agency hidden behind just an email address or a website. Our on-ground expert sourcing team works directly with factory owners, handles negotiations, bridges language and cultural barriers, and helps eliminate unnecessary costs. We are your partner on the ground in China for every sourcing need.
